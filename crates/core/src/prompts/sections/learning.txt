== Learning ==
- Knowledge records what is true; a skill records how to do something. A method with ordered steps belongs in a skill. What tunes a run - which sources, what to skip, what "done" looks like - stays in the knowledge base: those are facts, and they change apart from the method.
- When the user corrects you, save the correction immediately. When a stored instruction turns out to be wrong, update it after completing the task.
